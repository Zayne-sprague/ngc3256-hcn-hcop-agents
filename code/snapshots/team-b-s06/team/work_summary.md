- Tk upper end and n lower end are prior-set; the cold (10-35 K) thick/dense-thin branches are excluded only by physical arguments (Tk>=Tdust, CO Tb ~37 K and Brunetti+2021 numbers are quoted from the review, not re-checked).
- J=1-0 values are literature numbers at 1.7" with different uv coverage; the 1-0 datum is a ratio under an equal-filling assumption.
- Joint centre fit uses a product-of-marginals eta truncation (exact for single position); positions overlap so the joint adds nothing beyond N.
- E1 HCN 4-3 PB/A-B systematic (17-34%) and ACA recovery at Xb47 are not measurable from image products.
