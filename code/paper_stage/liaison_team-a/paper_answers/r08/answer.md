# r08: reconciling the 1-0 vs 3-2 contrast, 4-3, and the vib/H13CN limits

## 1. Best-supported D at matched beams
**D = contrast(3-2)/contrast(1-0) = 1.00 ± 0.05 (p0036, flux-complete 12m+7m) to 1.07 ± 0.05 (p0060, different estimator); I recommend quoting D = 1.0-1.1, i.e. 1.04 ± 0.07 (≤ 0.17 above 1 at 95%).**
- **2.2" beam:** 1-0 = 2015.1.00993 (complete flux); 3-2 = 2017.1.00379.S Xb4d (12m) + 3 Xb4f 7m EBs. N/S HCN/HCO+ is 1.467±0.022 at 1-0 and 1.441±0.023 at 3-2, so D = 0.98 ± 0.02 (±350 km/s) or 1.04 ± 0.02 (±150 km/s). Source: /snapshots/team-b-s21/out/q34/s192.txt. The adopted error ±0.05 includes the 12m+7m vs 12m-only systematic.
- **1.5" beam:** 1-0 = 2015.1.00412 (X145); same 3-2 data. D = 0.95 ± 0.04 / 1.01 ± 0.03. The contrast is 1.62 (1-0) and 1.53-1.67 (3-2).
- **2.5" beam (p0060):** 1-0 = X961 convolved; 3-2 = 12m+7m. Over 144 variants (beam, window, baseline, estimator, uv filter) D = 1.07 ± 0.05 (0.97-1.20). Estimator choice alone moves it between 1.0 and 1.15 (/snapshots/team-b-s31/out/q56/s345.txt).
- Team-a image-plane check (p0059, /snapshots/team-a-s32/out/q55/r10_core.csv) gives 1-0 N/S 1.45±0.22 at 1.0" (2016.1.00965) and 1.44±0.09 at 2.5".

**Why p0059's "50-75% (log) of the contrast is at 1-0" differs from D≈1.** It compared 1-0 from 2016.1.00965 (loses 25-45% of the flux, so a lower limit) with the 3-2 N/S = 2.08 (1.1") and 1.81 (2.5") measured on the 12m-only Xb4d image. Xb4d loses far more flux at S than at N: the 12m+7m/12m recovery at 2.2" is HCN S 1.34 vs N 1.06 (HCO+ 1.17 vs 1.07), which inflates the 3-2 contrast (Xb4d-only 1.65 vs 1.44 at 2.2"). With flux-complete data and matched beams the **whole 3-2 contrast is already at 1-0 (ln ratio ≈ 100% ± ~10%)**. That 50-75% figure is superseded by p0036/p0060. I flag this as an error in the p0059 wording, not in the data. Corrected value: fraction of ln(3-2 contrast) present at 1-0 = 0.9-1.0 (2.2"/1.5"), 0.8-1.0 (2.5").

**Matched ~1" has no clean answer:** no flux-complete 1-0 at 1" exists (00965 loses flux; 1" 3-2 needs 12m+7m at a 1" taper, and the X94a 1" 3-2 combination was not done: p0036 open problem). So 1.5" is the tightest flux-complete match.

**Do I endorse "the contrast is J-independent within 5-10%"?** Yes for 1-0 vs 3-2 at ≥1.5" resolution (D = 1.00-1.07 ± 0.05; 95% limit <0.17). Caveats: (i) image-plane beam matching, not strict common-uv; no kλ cut; (ii) the 1" core is not tested; (iii) the contrast itself depends on estimator/aperture at the 10% level (1-0: 1.53 pixel vs 1.29-1.40 at r=FWHM); (iv) 4-3 is excluded from the claim (below); (v) it does not decide excitation vs abundance: ~7% of RADEX same-A pairs reach 1-0 contrast ≥1.35, so excitation-only is disfavoured, not excluded (p0060, p0059).

## 2. 4-3 N/S contrast (1.6 vs 3.7): NOT resolved
- 3.7: HCN 4-3 (2018.1.00493) / HCO+ 4-3 (2018.1.00223) at 1.0" (N 0.814±0.019, S 0.222±0.015; 3.3 after the missing-flux correction); 3.6 at 2.5". Source: /workspace/out/q53/fixedwin_all.csv, fixedwin43.csv.
- 1.6: single-observation Xb47 (both lines in one tuning) at 4.5" (N 0.515, S 0.317); here N and S blend in the 4.5" beam.
- The S HCN 4-3 flux in the high-resolution data is 0.61 of Xb47 (HCO+ 0.87, N HCN 0.88): either extended S HCN is resolved out of the 0.9" data (→ 3.7 too high) or the Xb47 beam blends N and S (→ 1.6 too low). The settling observation is a one-tuning 4-3 HCN/HCO+ pair at ≤1". Status as in p0059/p0053 (S-below-SB at all beams, N SB-like at ≥188 pc). The 4-3 contrast should be quoted as 1.6-3.7, and the J-independence claim is only for J=1-0 vs 3-2.

## 3. Cross-check of limits quoted in p0057 / p0061
- **HCN-vib (p0057, team-b):** N L_vib <29 Lsun (48 pc), <52 (180 pc), <95 (1.1 kpc); S <25, <43, <102 (4-3: <207/<310 at 800 pc); Σ_vib <0.02 Lsun/pc² (≥60x below the CON criterion). **Agree with our own:** I re-ran the same method on the Xb4b spectra (/workspace/paper_answers/r06/fig6.py): alpha(3σ) N 0.033 / S 0.090 for the exp-linear model, matching s42's 0.035 / 0.09 for that model (the quoted 0.050 / 0.100 are the widest of the four wing models). Our own team-a statement (p0059) is Tvib < ~300-430 K, no CON-level pumping, not excluded: NGC 4418-like 200-300 K, or a <47 pc buried source diluted in the beam.
- **H13CN (p0061, team-b):** H13CN/HCN = 0.022±0.008 at N, <0.03 (3σ) at S in the common 2.2-2.5" beam from 2015.1.00993 (384-variant budget; N/S D=5.8 [2.1-13.5], 95% lower bound 1.2). **Consistent with team-a's** p0059 value (3 ± 3.5 for N/S) and with p0059's H13CN 4-3 limits alpha<0.10 (N) / <0.14 (S) (informative only at n≥1e6). Note the data are different (1-0 at 2.5" vs 4-3) and neither is decisive: opacity vs abundance remains undecided. p0059-r02 also shows H13CO+/HCO+ differs N/S (0.028±0.006 vs 0.013±0.006), so the 13C ratio difference is nuclear-wide, not HCN-specific. I did not re-measure H13CN 1-0 myself; I accept team-b's number as consistent with ours within errors but not independently reproduced.
