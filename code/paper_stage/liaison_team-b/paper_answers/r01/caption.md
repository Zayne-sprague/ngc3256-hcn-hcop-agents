**Figure 1.** Data overview of NGC 3256 at the common 0.26" (50 pc; 1" = 194 pc) circular beam, from ALMA 2017.1.00379.S execution Xb4b (Band 6). **(a)** HCO+ J=3-2 and **(b)** HCN J=3-2 peak brightness temperature T_b (RJ, K; maximum over |v − 2780| < 350 km/s of the 3-channel-smoothed spectrum; pixels below 3σ of the line-free rms, ≈0.07 K and 0.06 K, are black). **(c)** HCN/HCO+ T_b ratio from the moment-0 maps (window ±350 km/s about 2780 km/s), shown only where both lines exceed 3σ (σ_m0 ≈ 6.3 and 4.6 K km/s per pixel; grey elsewhere); colour scale 0-1.3. Crosses mark the N (−0.15", +2.55") and S (−0.15", −2.55") nuclei, with the Bridge (x) at the field centre (RA 156.96346°, Dec −43.90461°). Axes: arcsec offsets (east to the left) and pc (top and right). The circle is the 0.26" beam (native 0.246"×0.232", PA −49°, convolved to circular).
Values in a 0.13" radius around the markers (median): N peak T_b 1.67 (HCO+), 1.54 K (HCN), ratio 0.99; S 2.33, 1.05 K, ratio 0.48; Bridge 1.11, 0.53 K, ratio 0.51. The single-pixel/aperture values in `s171_summary.txt` (N 0.99, S 0.44 aperture; N 1.01, S 0.48 pixel; peak-channel T_b N 2.52/2.45 K, S 2.65/1.21 K for HCO+/HCN) are peak maxima, higher than these ring medians. The HCO+ moment-0 peaks lie at (E, N) = (0.00", +2.63") and (−0.19", −2.59"); the REGIONS table positions used elsewhere are N (0.0, +2.56") and S (−0.03", −2.56").
Processing: pbcor cubes → T_b → flux-conserving convolution to 0.26" → **constant baseline = per-pixel median of line-free channels (v_rel < −450 or > 600 km/s)** (not a linear baseline, as the request stated; baseline choice changes R by ≤0.01) → HCN spectrum interpolated onto the HCO+ velocity grid → moments. Noise from line-free channels. Absolute flux scale uncertainty 14% (10% per line) applies to each ratio, not to the N/S contrast. Script: `fig1_overview.py` (pipeline of `s170_q26_ratios.py`); numbers in `fig1_numbers.txt`.
