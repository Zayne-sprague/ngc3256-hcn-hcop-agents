**Figure F3b** (`f3b_ns_species.png/.pdf`; values `f3b_values.csv`; script `f3b_plot.py`; inputs `/workspace/out/q52/s320.json` and `s322.txt` from `scripts/s320_q52_chem_ratios.py`, `s322_q52_cont_ref.py`; p0058).
Left, log y: N/S ratio of the integrated line brightness (K km/s, +-350 km/s, single pixel at fixed N and S positions, linear baseline) for HCO+, HCN, HNC, CN. Filled green squares = J=1-0 at a common 2.5" circular beam: 1.42/2.21/2.69/2.98 (stat +-0.01/0.03/0.13/0.03). Filled blue circles = J=3-2 at 1.4": 1.45/3.11/2.67/3.73 (stat +-0.01/0.04/0.16/0.08). Dashed lines = N/S of the 1.1 mm continuum (Xb4d spw25_27_29_31, 12-m, same single-pixel method): **1.25 at 1.4" and 1.54 at 2.5"** (N 64.4/S 51.3 mK; 33.6/21.8 mK). Open symbols = line N/S divided by continuum N/S of the matching beam, i.e. line per unit continuum (a dust-column proxy): 1-0 0.92/1.43/1.75/1.93, 3-2 1.16/2.49/2.14/2.98. Shaded bars = effect of a dust temperature ratio Td_N/Td_S between 0.5 and 2: in the Rayleigh-Jeans limit (h nu/k = 12.4 K at 258 GHz, so only approximate for cold dust) continuum N/S = (dust column N/S) x (Td_N/Td_S), hence the line-per-dust-column N/S = open-symbol value x Td_N/Td_S, i.e. multiplied by 0.5 to 2 (HCN 3-2: 1.2-5.0). Right: N/S of (line/HCO+), which equals the continuum-normalised N/S relative to HCO+ and is independent of continuum and Td: 1-0 HCN 1.56+-0.03, HNC 1.90+-0.09, CN 2.10+-0.03; 3-2 2.14+-0.03, 1.84+-0.11, 2.56+-0.06.
Errors: line-free-channel stat only (rms x sqrt(N_ch) x dv). Systematics not in the bars: window (+-250 vs +-350 km/s: <=3%), beam and independent-project cross-check (<=5%, p0058), flux scale (10% per band, partly cancels in N/S), single-pixel position (S position jackknife ~10% in S line, p0060), and no error on the continuum N/S (single pixel, 12-m only).
**Not uv-matched (all entries):** the five lines and the continuum come from different projects/arrays. 1-0 HCN, HCO+, HNC: 2015.1.00993 X961 (short-spacing complete); 1-0 CN: 2018.1.00223 X4d. 3-2 HCN, HCO+ and the 1.1 mm continuum: 2017.1.00379 Xb4d, **12-m only** (loses extended flux, more at S); 3-2 HNC: 2015.1.00412 X169 (12-m); 3-2 CN (Band 7 complex, one window): 2018.1.00493 X1df1 (12-m). In particular the 1-0 (flux-complete) line N/S is divided by a 12-m-only continuum N/S, and the 3-2 HNC/HCN at 1.4" (0.86, N/S) is a recovery artefact (1.17 at 2.5", p0058). The 3-2 values at 1.4" are 12-m-only and have larger S flux loss than the 1-0 ones. The N/S continuum ratio itself grows with beam (1.07 at 1.1", 1.25 at 1.4", 1.54 at 2.5").
**Non-dust continuum at S:** if part of the 1.1 mm continuum at S is free-free or synchrotron/AGN emission, the dust continuum at S is lower than measured, the dust N/S is higher, and the line-per-dust N/S is **lower** (the S deficit is smaller), not larger; p0058's wording ("would enlarge the deficit") has the wrong sign (see answer.md). The HCO+-relative panel is unaffected.
