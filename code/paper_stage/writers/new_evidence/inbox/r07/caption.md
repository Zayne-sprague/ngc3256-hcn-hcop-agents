**Figure F3a** (`f3a_literature_context.png/.pdf`; table `f3a_literature_table.csv`; script `f3a_plot.py`, literature transcription `snapshots/team-b-s28/scripts/s300_q46_lit_context.py`, checked against the paper text).
**Left:** the 21 galaxies of Nishimura+2024 (A&A 686, A48; CON-quest II) with both HCN and HCO+ J=3-2 detected, ranked by the *resolved* HCN/HCO+ L' (= brightness-temperature) ratio measured in a beam-sized aperture at ~0.3" (beams 0.28-0.42", 50-423 pc; Table 4 ratios with their 1-sigma errors, Table 2 beams). Colour = bolometric AGN fraction alpha_AGN (Diaz-Santos+2017, as in Nishimura Table 1): <0.10 (12 galaxies), 0.10-0.25 (7), >=0.25 (IRAS F05189-2524, 0.60), unknown (UGC 11763). Marker = star: CON (Table 1: IRAS 17208-0014, IRAS 17578-0400, ESO 320-G030); diamond: HCN-vib detected but not CON (IRAS F14378-3651, ESO 173-G015, IRAS F05189-2524 [faint]); circle: HCN-vib not detected; square: unclear (NGC 6240, broad HCO+ complicates the HCN-vib evaluation). NGC 5734 (HCN only, ratio > 2.5) is shown as an arrow and not ranked; UGC 2982 (neither line) is omitted. Vertical lines: NGC 3256 N and S from p0031/p0055 (Xb4b/Xb4d, aperture r = beam FWHM, T_b): **N 0.99 (0.28", 55 pc) and 0.69 (1.29", 250 pc); S 0.44 and 0.37**. Percentile = fraction of the 21 with a lower ratio: **N 55 pc 38th (8/21), N 250 pc 19th (4/21), S 55 pc and 250 pc: 0th, i.e. below all 21** (the lowest, ESO 148-IG002, is 0.445+-0.015, equal to S 0.44 within errors; with the NGC 5734 limit counted as a 22nd: 36th, 18th, 0, 0). Sample median 1.13 (range 0.45-2.67). The comparison is at matched *physical* scale (55 vs 50-420 pc), not matched angular beam for the 250 pc values (1.29" vs 0.3"); 8 of the 21 have beams <110 pc (median 1.08).
**Right:** Izumi+2016 (ApJ 818, 42, Table 1) high-resolution (<500 pc) HCN/HCO+ J=4-3 bands: starburst nuclei 0.24-1.03 (M82, NGC 253, NGC 1097 ring, NGC 1614, NGC 7469 B/C/D), AGN 1.11-3.19 (NGC 1068 x5, NGC 1097, NGC 7469). NGC 3256 N (red) and S (black) 4-3 **at different beams, which are not comparable with each other**: team-b p0055 (Xb47 ACA, 4.14"x2.70" = 800x520 pc, peak pixel, +-350 km/s, T_b): N 0.52, S 0.29 (N and S 1.5" apart are partly blended in this beam); team-a p0053 (12-m cubes of 2018.1.00493 HCN x 2018.1.00223 HCO+ smoothed to circular beams): 1.0" (188 pc) N 0.81+-0.02, S 0.22+-0.02 (stat); 1.9" (357 pc) N 0.67, S 0.18; the 56 pc core values (N 0.95 [0.81-1.12], S 0.29 [0.25-0.35], open symbols) are an **extrapolation**, not a measurement. The p0053 cubes lack short spacings (S HCN recovered 61%, N 88% vs same-project ACA), so p0053's S is biased low and N/S (3.7 at 1.0") is an array/beam effect (p0066; not reproduced by team-b, whose matched-beam 12-m value is 2.7); the ACA values have no such loss. All NGC 3256 4-3 points lie in the SB range.
