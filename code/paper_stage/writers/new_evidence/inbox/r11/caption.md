**Figure (estimator_spread.png).** N/S contrast of HCN/HCO+ 3-2 (ratio of the N and S T_b line ratios, window ±350 km/s about 2780 km/s, line-free baseline) versus estimator, for circular uv-tapered beams of 1.5" (left) and 2.2" (right). Estimators: peak pixel at the HCO+ 3-2 moment-0 peak within 0.4" of N and S, and apertures of radius 0.25", 0.5", 0.75" and the beam FWHM (1.5" or 2.2"), all centred on that pixel. Blue: 12m+7m; orange: 12m-only (uv-tapered cubes from `scripts/s191_q34_casa_image.py`, 0.3" cells; sub-pixel apertures by cubic interpolation). Green dashed (left): Xb4b 0.24" restored cube convolved in the image plane to 1.5". Error bars are 1-sigma from the line-free channels. At 1.5" (12m+7m): 1.79 (peak), 1.76, 1.73, 1.70, 1.54 (r=1.5"); 12m-only: 1.90, 1.87, 1.83, 1.78, 1.59; Xb4b convolved: 2.03, 2.00, 1.98, 1.95, 1.83. At 2.2" (12m+7m): 1.66, 1.67, 1.67, 1.66, 1.45; 12m-only: 1.69, 1.70, 1.69, 1.67, 1.46. The N–S separation is 1.45", so the drop at r = beam FWHM is aperture overlap, not dilution within the beam. The shaded band marks the beam-radius estimator. Script: `r11_fig.py`.
