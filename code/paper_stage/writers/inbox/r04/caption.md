**Figure 4.** Test of excitation / column / abundance explanations for the N/S HCN/HCO+ contrast in a matched 2.2" circular beam (±150 km/s; HCN, HCO+, H13CN, H13CO+ 1-0 from 2015.1.00993.S, 3-2 from 12m+7m 2017.1.00379.S) with the joint one-zone RADEX fit of `s353/s354/s356_q57*` (p0062). **Observed contrast N/S**: 1-0 **1.45 ± 0.01** (stat), 3-2 **1.50 ± 0.02** (stat) (`fig4_r3.py` from `out/q29/s183.json`, `out/q34/s192.json`); the fit uses 5% cross-array scale and 10% model errors, so the effective uncertainty is larger than the statistical bar.
**Left:** Δχ² (relative to the full model Hfull) as a function of the model 1-0 contrast when the abundance ratio N(HCN)/N(HCO+) is forced equal at N and S: excitation-only (Hexc, blue) and column-only (Hcol, orange); solid X = 12C/13C 40-100, dashed X = 20-200 (profile computed at 1.05, 1.10-1.15, 1.20, 1.38, 1.58 only, so lines are piecewise). The curves reach Δχ² = 5-8 near 1.1-1.2 (minimum 7.7 Hexc / 5.0 Hcol for X 40-100) and 10-18 at the observed 1.45. Symbols at the best fit: abundance-only Habun (Δχ² 3.7, contrast 1.33) and Hfull (0, 1.29). Best-fit contrasts, 1-0: Hexc 1.09, Hcol 1.13, Habun 1.33, Hfull 1.29. **3-2 (profile likelihood not computed, best fits only)**: Hexc 1.23, Hcol 1.35, Habun 1.57, Hfull 1.53 vs observed 1.50. (X 40-100 default errors; with the physical priors the minima are Hexc 7.7, Hcol 6.7 (5.0 unconstrained) — same conclusion.)
**Right:** ln of the Bayes factor Habun/Hexc for three error models (halved: 5%→2.5%, 10%→5%; default; doubled) and priors (X 40-100 or 20-200; unconstrained or physical: log n ≥ 3.8, L ≤ 100 pc, X(HCO+) ≤ 1e-8).
