**Figure (R1).** HCN/HCO+ J=3-2 brightness-temperature (RJ) ratio and T_b profiles in annuli around the N and S nuclei (annuli 0-0.25", 0.25-0.5", 0.5-1", 1-2", 2-3"; centres = HCO+ 3-2 moment peak of the 0.26" Xb4b map, same centre for all runs; 1" = 194 pc). Left: ratio (N filled/solid, S open/dashed). Middle/right: annulus-mean integrated T_b (±350 km/s about 2780 km/s) of HCN (solid) and HCO+ (dotted) at N and S. Blue: Xb4b convolved to a circular 0.26" beam (matched native); orange: Xb4b convolved to 1.0"; green: Xb4d at the same 1.0" beam (uv/flux cross-check). Pipeline = s170/s171 (flux-conserving convolution of T_b, constant per-pixel baseline from line-free channels, HCN on the HCO+ velocity grid). Error bars: statistical only (line-free-channel noise incl. spatial correlation); the 14% flux-scale error applies to absolute ratios, not to N/S comparisons. Grey vertical lines mark the beam FWHM.
Key numbers (Xb4b 0.26"): N 1.00±0.02 (0-0.25"), 0.88±0.02, 0.65±0.01, 0.43±0.02, 0.38±0.05 (2-3"); S 0.44±0.01, 0.39±0.02, 0.33±0.02, 0.34±0.03, 0.45±0.03 (2-3", outer annulus; see answer.md). At 1.0" the N ratio is 0.84 → 0.37 and S flat at 0.36-0.43. Xb4b−Xb4d at 0.5-1.5" (1.0" beam): N 0.631 vs 0.656, S 0.353 vs 0.376.
