**Figure 2.** HCN/HCO+ J=3-2 brightness-temperature ratio and peak brightness temperature of the N and S nuclei (and the Bridge) as a function of beam size, NGC 3256 (ALMA 2017.1.00379.S; 1" = 194 pc).
**(a)** Ratio of HCN to HCO+ T_b (RJ, same ±350 km/s window about 2780 km/s, equal circular beams) in an aperture of radius equal to the beam FWHM centred on the HCO+ 3-2 peak (the estimator of `s171_summary.txt`, Table 1). Filled circles: Xb4b at 0.26", 0.28", 0.62", 1.29" (50, 55, 120, 250 pc); open squares: Xb4d at 1.29" (250 pc); open triangles: Xb4d and Xb4f at 6.5" (offset in x for clarity; N, S, Bridge from left to right). Error bars: statistical (line-free channels; mostly smaller than the symbols). Grey bands: ±14% flux-scale uncertainty on the N and S ratios (10% per line); this scale cancels in N/S. N/S contrast (aperture): 2.25 (0.26"), 2.32 (0.28"), 2.11 (0.62"), 1.90 (1.29"); Xb4d 1.84 (1.29") and 1.10 (6.5"); Xb4f 1.07 (6.5"). Adopted 2.1 ± 0.2 at 50-120 pc; the spread among aperture, same-pixel and peak-channel estimators is 1.9-2.3 (≈2.0-2.3 at 50-120 pc, 1.8-2.1 at 250 pc). The contrast is also beam dependent: R_N falls from 0.99 to 0.69 between 55 and 250 pc while R_S changes from 0.43 to 0.37. The 6.5" points are blends affected by differential uv-flux loss in the compact arrays. The 12m+7m uv-matched 2.2" contrast (1.44-1.50) is not shown (see r07).
**(b)** Peak T_b (K, RJ; 3-channel-smoothed peak channel at the aperture-centre pixel) of HCO+ (solid) and HCN (dashed, open symbols) at N (orange) and S (blue); same symbols/x-axis as (a). At 0.26" the HCO+ peak is equal at N and S (2.52 vs 2.65 K, N/S 0.95) while HCN is 2.0× lower at S (1.21 vs 2.45 K, N/S 2.03). The equal-HCO+ / lower-HCN pattern holds at 55 pc (N/S HCO+ 0.90, HCN 2.01) and 120 pc (0.99, 2.02); at 250 pc HCO+ N/S is 1.37.
The inset (ratio vs radius) was not made: no radial-profile output was available without new analysis.
Colours: Okabe-Ito. Script: `fig2_scale.py` (inputs `out/q26/s170_Xb4{b,d,f}.json`).
