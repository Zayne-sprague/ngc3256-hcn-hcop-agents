# STORY (lead author) — NGC 3256 HCN/HCO+ at 50 pc

## QUESTION
Is an elevated HCN/HCO+ line ratio a signature of AGN heating in dusty nuclei, and what sets the HCN/HCO+ ratio in the two
nuclei of the merger NGC 3256 (N: starburst; S: low-luminosity, obscured AGN)?
Believed/debated: ALMA and single-dish surveys report HCN/HCO+ (3-2, 4-3) higher in AGN than in starbursts, the "submm
HCN enhancement" (Izumi+2016, 2016ApJ...818...42I; Imanishi+2016, 2016AJ....152..218I), but other studies find no
trend with AGN luminosity or star-formation rate, and attribute high ratios to abundance, shock heating or
compact-obscured-nucleus (CON) vibrational pumping (Nishimura+2024, 2024A&A...686A..48N; Butterworth+2022,
2022A&A...667A.131B; Aalto+2015, 2015A&A...584A..42A). For NGC 3256, Harada+2018 (2018ApJ...855...49H) saw no
enhancement at the AGN at 1.7" (~330 pc), Michiyama+2018 (2018ApJ...868...95M) gave 1-0 values. Unknown: whether the
ratio at ~50 pc, where nuclei are separable, differs between the nuclei, in which line the difference lies, and whether
excitation (J, IR pumping), opacity or abundance produce it.

## ANSWER (title)
"The AGN nucleus is the low one: HCN/HCO+ 3-2 is about twice as high in the starburst nucleus of NGC 3256 as at its AGN on
50-200 pc scales, because HCN (with HNC and CN) is weak relative to HCO+ at the AGN; no vibrational-pumping signature is seen"
Key numbers (T_b ratios, 0.26" = 50 pc): N 0.99, S 0.44, contrast 2.1 +/- 0.2 at 50-120 pc (stat ~0.05, estimator spread
+/-0.1), 2.0 at 1.0", 1.5 (flux-complete 12m+7m) at 2.2"; the 14% flux scale cancels. The uv term at <=0.6" is untested and
one-sided (can lower 2.1 by up to ~0.3, as seen at 1.5-2.2"). HCO+ peak T_b equal (2.75 vs 2.81 K), HCN differs 2.0x;
HNC/HCO+ and CN/HCO+ N/S contrasts are the same as HCN/HCO+ (~2.1-2.2 at 1.1", 3-2; 1.9-2.1 at 1-0 2.5"). At >=1.5" the
contrast is the same at 1-0 and 3-2 (D = 1.04 +/- 0.07).
What is NOT established: the cause. Excitation-only is disfavoured (~2 sigma, 2.2" beam, one-zone fits); an abundance excess of
the N-bearing family at N is favoured but tentative; 12C/13C and opacity are not separated; the 2.1 core contrast itself has
not been tested at 1-0 or 4-3.

## SIGNIFICANCE
Published NGC 3256 ratios are 1.7" (~330 pc) blends in which N and S differ by only ~1.5 and "no enhancement at the AGN" was
noted (Harada+2018, 2018ApJ...855...49H); Michiyama+2018 (2018ApJ...868...95M) gave 1-0 values at ~2". Nishimura+2024
(2024A&A...686A..48N) found 21 resolved U/LIRG ratios (0.45-2.67, mean ~1.1) with no trend in AGN luminosity and high ratios
in shock-heated outflows. This paper adds the within-galaxy, same-array, same-tuning comparison at 50 pc: the starburst N core
(~1.0) is an ordinary LIRG nucleus (38th percentile of Nishimura+2024), whereas the AGN nucleus S (0.44) sits at the very bottom
of that distribution (tied with ESO 148-IG002, 0.445; not a significant "below minimum"), with HCO+ brightness identical. So a
single-J HCN/HCO+ ratio does not flag, and here inverts, the AGN/starburst order. New elements: (i) the N HCN-rich ~100 pc core,
(ii) the contrast sits in HCN, HNC and CN together relative to HCO+ (a nitrogen-species signature, not an HCN-specific
X-ray/IR effect), (iii) the HCN v2=1 limits. Caveat for the claim: S is a low-luminosity, energetically minor AGN, so this
is a counterexample to a general AGN signature, not a statement that AGN never raise HCN/HCO+. We cannot verify "first"
(removed from the lead author's wording).

- Claim: (a) the N/S contrast is the same at 1-0 and 3-2 at matched >=1.5" flux-complete beams (D = 1.00+/-0.05 with 12m+7m; 1.07+/-0.05
  in the systematics budget; adopted 1.04+/-0.07;  the earlier 1.10 and the HCN 3-2/1-0 S/N = 0.71 were uv-incompleteness artefacts, S/N = 0.93+/-0.05). This is the envelope/blend contrast (~1.5), NOT a test of the 2.1 core: no 1-0 at <=1" is flux-complete; 4-3 N/S is
  unresolved (1.6 at 4.5" blend vs 3.3-3.7 at 1.0"), and if the 3.3-3.7 holds the contrast RISES with J in the core (request r10);
  (b) no HCN v2=1 emission (Sigma_vib < 0.02 Lsun/pc2, L_vib < 29 Lsun at N, <25 at S, >=60x below the Falstad+2021 Sigma

  alpha<0.04) or a <47 pc buried source diluted in the beam; the S vib/HCN L' limit (<0.085-0.10) does not lie below the lower CON

## LIMITS
- The 2.1 core contrast is measured only in 3-2 with 12m-only data at <=0.6"; its uv term is untested (up to -0.3). J-independence (D=1.04+/-0.07) holds only for flux-complete >=1.5" beams, so it does not constrain excitation in the N core; 4-3 N/S is 1.6-3.7 (unresolved) and may indicate J-dependence.
- The pumping limits exclude CON-level pumping only; moderate pumping (Tvib 150-300 K) or a <47 pc buried source is not excluded, and the S vib/HCN limit does not reach the lower CON range.
- Does not establish the cause: abundance (HCN-family excess at N) vs 12C/13C vs a mixture of (T,n) differences are not
  separated; model preference is ~2 sigma, depends on error model and the 12C/13C prior; one-zone/two-phase RADEX only.
- Opacity unresolved: H13CN/HCN at S is only a limit (<0.03); N detection 3 sigma. Would need ~10x deeper 1-0 H13CN/H13CO+ at
  ~2" (sigma ~0.003) or HN13C/HNC, 13CO/C18O in matched beams.
- Temperature of the compact N core is unconstrained; HCN 4-3/3-2 (and CH3CN/CH3OH/H2CO thermometers) at <=0.5" would decide.
- uv matching is image-plane only; 4-3 N/S contrast unresolved (1.6 vs 3.7); 1-0 contrast depends on aperture (1.3-1.5).
- Single-galaxy; AGN at S is energetically minor (low-luminosity), so the result says the ratio is not a *general* AGN
  signature, not that AGNs never enhance HCN. 14% flux scale and D=40 Mpc (1" = 194 pc) are assumptions.
- A true AGN-vs-SB calibration needs resolved ratios plus 13C species in a sample of AGN with known luminosities.
