# Response to the referee report (revision 4, with the new evidence round)

Summary: the story is kept (profile, contrast, species, pumping limit, cause open) and the new round settles the main open point: the 2.0–2.3 contrast at 50–200 pc survives missing flux and beam matching in the image plane. It is not uv-confirmed (long-baseline visibilities could not be restored) and the paper says so. Two old numbers are revised (below). No further requests are needed (`requests.json` is `[]`).
