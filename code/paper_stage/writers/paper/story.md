# STORY (lead author, revision 4, after the new evidence round) — NGC 3256 HCN/HCO+ at 50 pc

## QUESTION
Is an elevated HCN/HCO+ J=3-2 ratio a signature of AGN heating in a dusty nucleus? Tested within NGC 3256 (N: starburst; S: obscured,
low-luminosity AGN) at 50-250 pc, comparing two nuclei of one galaxy with the same arrays and tuning. Debated: submm "HCN enhancement"
in AGN (Izumi+2016; Imanishi+2016) vs no trend with AGN luminosity and shock, abundance or vibrational-pumping alternatives
(Nishimura+2024; Butterworth+2022; Aalto+2015). Harada+2018 at 1.7" (330 pc): N/S 1.49+-0.03 at 1-0 (recomputed from their Table 13), ~1.9 at 3-2 (transcribed, not re-checked).

## ANSWER (title unchanged: a description, not a cause)
"The HCN/HCO+ J=3-2 ratio rises inward at the starburst nucleus of NGC 3256 but stays low at its low-luminosity AGN."
N rises from 0.38 (2-3") to 1.00 (<0.25", 49 pc); S stays at 0.33-0.44. The N/S contrast is 2.26 (+0.11/-0.15) at 0.3" and 2.0+-0.1 at 1"
in the image plane. NEW: it is not a missing-flux artefact there (Xb4b vs Xb4d recover both lines equally to 2%; 12-m vs 12-m+7-m at
1.0/1.5" changes N/S by <2%; clean residual/restored bias <=3.5%), but it is not uv-confirmed (Xb4b visibilities cannot be restored) and nothing
inside 0.15" is supported. It falls with beam size (dilution explains 45-73% of the drop to 430 pc) to 1.7 (overlap-free apertures) or 1.44-1.54
(beam-sized aperture, lowered by N-S aperture overlap) at 430 pc in flux-complete data, same at J=1-0 and 3-2 (D 0.95-1.06) and between 3-2 and 4-3
with the 7-m array (1.02+-0.18 at 6.5"). No CON-level vibrational HCN at either nucleus. Cause (abundance, opacity, 12C/13C, excitation) NOT
separated. So in this galaxy the ratio does not flag the AGN (one galaxy, energetically minor AGN); N is an ordinary value, S is among the lowest.

## REVISIONS TO NUMBERS
- Core contrast: was "2.1, upper estimate, missing-flux term untested"; now 2.26 (+0.11/-0.15) at 0.3", 2.0+-0.1 at 1" with a measured recovery test (image plane).
- Flux-complete contrast at 430 pc: was "1.44, equal to the published 1.5"; now 1.44 is the beam-aperture value (overlap-lowered), overlap-free 1.66-1.70.
- Missing-flux allowance "-0.2 to -0.3" (7-m at 1.5-2.2") withdrawn: the 7-m effect proper is -0.01 to -0.11; the rest is an unexplained convolution-vs-uv-taper term (0.1-0.27 at 1.5").
- 4-3: "unresolved 1.6-3.7" replaced: ACA-visible visibility-plane contrast 1.8 (+0.3/-0.2 sys, +-0.12 stat; no scale); the 3.7 at 1" is an array/beam effect (12-m resolves out S HCN); 1.5x flux offset was a baseline artefact.
- S kinetic temperature: not measured (CO lower limit 30-40 K; one-zone fits 17-30 K; unreconciled).

## SIGNIFICANCE
New relative to Harada+2018 (1.7", N/S 1.49 at 1-0) and Ledger+2021 (2.2"): the 50 pc profile (N rising, S flat); the contrast at 50-200 pc (2.2, 2.0)
with tests against differential flux loss and beam matching, and its beam dependence; J-independence at 430 pc and species family; CON-level pumping limit.
Not new: the 1.5-1.7 contrast at 330-430 pc. Within-galaxy counter-example to a general AGN signature, consistent with no trend in Nishimura+2024
(definitions differ; indicative). Not a statement about all AGN; cannot say whether the AGN lowers S.

## MAIN RESULTS (one claim each)
R1 (3.1): N ratio rises inward (0.38 -> 1.00), S stays low; inner profile reproduced in a second 0.30" analysis.
R2 (3.2): contrast 2.26 at 0.3", 2.0 at 1" (image plane, flux-loss tests), falls to 1.5-1.7 at 430 pc; J-independent where tested.
R3 (3.3): HCN, HNC and CN are all weak against HCO+ at S.
R4 (3.4): no CON-level vibrational HCN (alpha<0.05 N, <0.10 S).
R5 (3.5): cause not separated; fit does not reproduce 1.45; thin-line contrast ~2.5 is a hint at most.
Appendices: A flux recovery, wings/baseline, temperatures, J-ladder/4-3, fits; B conventions and tables; C provenance.

## LIMITS
- Sub-arcsec contrast image-plane only; Xb4b visibilities lost (OOM x4); deconvolution bias at 0.3" unbounded; high-pass bound 2.6-3.1 pessimistic; uv N amplitude excess 1.3-1.5 at 90-200 kl unexplained.
- Convolution-vs-uv-taper term 0.1-0.27 at 1.5" unexplained.
- 4-3: no flux-complete core contrast; 12-m 4-3 S biased low.
- Pumping: CON-level only; S limit overlaps CON range; buried compact source open.
- Cause not established; Delta chi2 5-8 weak; BF 2.6-2200 over 48 cells.
- Single galaxy; 14% flux scale and D=40 Mpc assumptions; Falstad+2021 inclusion of NGC 3256 disputed between literature checks (not used).
