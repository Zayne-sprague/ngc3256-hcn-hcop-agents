# Q28 work summary: two-phase RADEX model of N and S
**Script**: scripts/s360_q28_twophase.py (args SIG10 SIG22 SIG13 X TAUMAX; ~1 min per run), scripts/s361_update_report_q28.py (report). **Outputs**: out/q28/s360_*.txt/json (7 variants).
**Method**: extended s108 idea. Grid out/q3d (RADEX LVG, dv=150 km/s shared by both phases). Compact phase (Tk,n,N(HCO+),A=N(HCN)/N(HCO+), Gaussian size theta) + extended thin phase (tau<=1, fills all beams). Data per nucleus: 10" ladders HCN/HCO+ 2-1,3-2,4-3; 2.2" 1-0 and 3-2 (Q29/Q34); 1.29" 3-2 T_b and 0.24" floors (p0026/s160); H13CN limit (tau43<X*0.128). Compared with (a) one Gaussian source, (b) one gas state with two source sizes. Evidence by flat-grid sums.
**Results**
- Two-phase fits: chi2 8.7 (N), 6.4 (S) vs 157/177 (single Gaussian) and 52/29 (one state, two sizes). ln BF 67/79 vs (a); 16.8/6.9 vs (b); over 7 error/X/tau variants, never below 4.6 (S, 2-phase vs one state).
- Compact: FWHM 0.5" N (0.3-0.5), 0.3" S (0.15-0.5) (~100 / ~60 pc), eta_c(10")=2.5e-3/0.9e-3, carries 25% (N) / 11% (S) of the 10" HCN 3-2; N(HCO+) 3e15-1e16, log n 4.75 (N) / 5.75 (S), thick. N/S contrast sits in compact A (N logA 0.5, S 0.0, each wide); extended A equal.
- Compact Tk unconstrained: P(Tk<=30)=0.18-0.55 N, 0.46-0.78 S vs prior 0.33. The p0012 cool S core (20-30 K) is allowed, mildly favoured at S (odds ~1.3-3.6), not required.
- Extended thin phase: Tk 112 K (56-224), P(Tk>50)>=0.99 in all variants, n~3e4, N(HCO+)~3e13. So Q40's Tk>~50 K is not a mixing artefact of the cold core; the extended phase itself needs Tk>50 K (n<1e5).
- Sigma_gas(10")=890/910 Msun/pc2 at X(HCO+)=1e-9 (300 at 3e-9, 90 at 1e-8), 80% in the extended phase. Dust bound 14-77 needs X(HCO+)>=1.0-1.8e-8 (5.5-9.6e-8 for 14): tension. X(HCO+) cannot be measured (H13CO+ only at 2.2", no N(H2)).
**Literature**: Harada+18 (nuclear ratios, 1.7"), Michiyama+18 (S outflow, 2-phase), Tang+19 (12C/13C 40-100) as context; p0012, p0026, p0042, Q27 s108, Q40, Q57 reused.
**Uncertain**: coarse grid (extended phase on 1-2 grid points), extended filling fixed to 1, floors lower limits only, ±150 km/s boxes vs 150 km/s model, calibration across projects 10-15% assumed; compact Tk prior-limited.
